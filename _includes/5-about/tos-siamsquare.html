SiamSquare Portal and SiamSquare Survey! application

We also operate the SiamSquare survey platform, which client companies use to run surveys:

SiamSquare Portal (siamsquare.org), where companies design questionnaires, manage their team and interviewer logins, and view and download results. Its web survey pages (siamsquare.org/r/) let respondents and interviewers answer a survey in a browser. SiamSquare Survey!, our mobile application covering both iOS and Android, which interviewers use to conduct either online or offline interviews with respondents.

This part of the terms applies to every company that uses these services, and to its portal users, interviewers and supervisors. By signing in to the portal, or by letting its team and interviewers use it, a company accepts these terms. They should be read with our privacy policy, which explains how personal information is handled.

Accounts and logins

A company is responsible for everyone it gives access to, and for what they do with it.

  • Each portal user signs in with their own account. Accounts and passwords must not be shared.
  • The company's managers decide who joins the company and with which role (manager, user or viewer). The role limits what each person can see and change.
  • Interviewer (PI) and supervisor (PS) logins are created in the portal for one survey. They work only in the application and the web survey, never in the portal. Each login should be given to one person, and disabled when that person no longer needs it.
  • Tell us straight away at support@siamsquare.org if you think an account or login has been misused.

Surveys and data

A company owns its questionnaires, survey answers, photos, audio, files and logo. We use them only to provide the service to that company, as described in our privacy policy, and we do not sell them or share them with other companies.

For its respondents' answers, the company is the "data controller" and we are the "data processor". The company is responsible for:

  • having the right to collect the data it asks for;
  • getting each respondent's consent before an interview, and telling them when audio, photos or location will be recorded;
  • handling respondents' requests to see, correct or delete their answers. We will help when asked.

Data stays until the company deletes it. Deleted files stay in the file bin for 7 days and are then removed for good, so anything the company needs to keep should be downloaded before it is deleted.

Survey assistant and document import

The survey assistant and document import use Anthropic's Claude service to suggest questions and turn documents into surveys. Only the questionnaire text is sent; respondents' answers are never sent.

Their suggestions can be wrong or incomplete. Every question, answer option and rule should be checked, and the survey tried in the portal's Test survey tab, before it goes live. The company is responsible for the final questionnaire.

Availability

We work to keep SiamSquare available and its data safe, but we cannot promise the service will never be interrupted. It may be unavailable during maintenance, or because of problems with the internet or our service providers.

Interviews done offline are kept in the application until the phone is online again, and are sent then. An interview is safely with us only once it has been sent, so interviewers should connect regularly and not delete the application while interviews are waiting.

Suspension and closing an account

A company can stop using SiamSquare at any time. It should download its results first, then ask us at support@siamsquare.org to close its accounts.

We may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms, puts other users or the platform at risk, or must be closed by law. Where we can, we will warn the company first and give it time to download its data.
