SiamSquare Portal and SiamSquare Survey! application

We also operate the SiamSquare survey platform, which client companies use to run surveys:

SiamSquare Portal (siamsquare.org), where companies design questionnaires, manage their team and interviewer logins, and view and download results. Its web survey pages (siamsquare.org/r/) let respondents and interviewers answer a survey in a browser. SiamSquare Survey!, our mobile application covering both iOS and Android, which interviewers use to conduct either online or offline interviews with respondents.

This part of the policy applies to everyone whose data passes through these services: portal users, interviewers and supervisors, and survey respondents. It is written to meet Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019) (PDPA).

Who's involved

The company that runs a survey decides what it asks and why, so it is responsible for its respondents' answers (the "data controller"). We store and process those answers on its behalf (the "data processor"). For portal accounts we are the data controller.

Consent and device permissions

Respondents take part by choice, and the company running the survey must get their consent before an interview starts. The company is responsible for telling respondents what the survey is for, and in particular when audio will be recorded. A respondent can stop an interview at any time.

Our legal basis under the PDPA is:

  • Consent, for respondents' answers, photos, audio and location.
  • Contract, for portal accounts and interviewer and supervisor logins, which are needed to provide the service.
  • Legitimate interest, for sign-in records and the activity log, which keep accounts secure.

Where we store it and how we protect it

The portal runs on Google Cloud in Bangkok, Thailand. Accounts, surveys and answers are kept in our central database, and photos, audio, logos and other files in Google Cloud Storage.

  • All traffic between the application, browsers and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Each company sees only its own surveys and data. Roles (admin, manager, user, viewer) limit what each person can open or change, and our servers check this on every request.
  • Portal passwords are stored hashed. Interviewer and supervisor passwords are also kept readable, so that the survey's owner, the company's managers and the admin can view them again; every view is recorded in the activity log.
  • Interviewers and supervisors can sign in only to the application and the web survey, never to the portal. A login can be disabled at any time, which ends its session.
  • Answers waiting on a phone (for example, an interview done offline) are stored inside the application and sent as soon as there is an internet connection.

Your rights

Under the PDPA you can ask us to:

  • see a copy of your personal information, or receive it in a common format;
  • correct it if it is wrong;
  • delete it, or limit its use;
  • stop using it, or withdraw your consent at any time.

Respondents should contact the company that ran the survey, which decides what happens to their answers. Its support email is shown in the application, and we will help that company carry out the request.

Portal users, interviewers and supervisors can contact us directly at support@siamsquare.org, or ask their company's manager. We answer requests within 30 days. You can also complain to Thailand's Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC).

Significant changes to how SiamSquare handles data are also announced in the portal before they take effect.
